# 3. Withdraw R-003 and P-003

## Status

Accepted (2026-09-25). Decided by the owner.

## Context

Checking the six clauses against the entry conditions in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) §2 before the first adoption found two problems.

- **R-003 "Change rules when the agent actually errs"** (certainty very low) mixed two statements. "Do not append feature descriptions as rules" is already said by R-002 and P-001. "Change rules when the agent errs" was its own content, supported only by [9], which could not separate rule changes from code changes. Its application ("check whether the change came from a real error") could not be checked mechanically.
- **P-003 "There is no evidence that repository overviews raise agent task success"** (certainty low) sat in `principles/` while stating that it did not meet the principle condition (moderate or higher). Task success is also out of scope (DESIGN.md §1).

## Decision

Withdraw both clauses and delete their files. The reasons live here; DESIGN.md §9 lists the clauses with a link.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep them `proposed` until their certainty rises.** Rejected by the owner: a clause that fails its layer's condition, left in that layer's folder, reads as breaking the layer rule.
- **Keep the files with a `deprecated` status.** Rejected: inert files mix into the clause list. `deprecated` was removed from the status values with this decision.
- **Keep only the "do not append" half of R-003.** Rejected: it duplicates R-002 and P-001.

## Consequences

- `practices/R-003-change-rules-when-the-agent-errs.md` and `principles/P-003-no-evidence-that-overviews-help.md` are deleted.
- The guide step that cited R-003 cites R-002 and P-001.
- The observation in [9] that only R-003 used is marked as no longer cited.
- [3], the source of P-003, remains only as supporting evidence in R-002.
