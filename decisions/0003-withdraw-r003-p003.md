# 3. Withdraw R-003 and P-003

## Status

Accepted (2026-09-25). The owner made this decision.

## Context

Before the first adoption, we examined the six clauses against the entry conditions in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) §2. Two clauses had problems.

- **R-003 "Change rules when the agent makes an error"** (certainty very low) contained two statements. R-002 and P-001 already say "Do not add feature descriptions as rules". The other statement had one source only, [9]. That study could not separate rule changes from code changes. Also, no check could verify the application of the clause.
- **P-003 "No evidence shows that repository overviews increase the task success of agents"** (certainty low) was in `principles/`. But the clause stated that it did not meet the principle condition, which is moderate or higher. Also, task success is out of scope (DESIGN.md §1).

## Decision

Withdraw the two clauses, and delete their files. This record keeps the reasons. DESIGN.md §9 lists the clauses and links to this record.

## Alternatives considered

- **Keep them with the status `proposed` until their certainty increases.** Rejected by the owner: a clause that does not meet the condition of its layer seems to break the layer rule.
- **Keep the files with the status `deprecated`.** Rejected: files with no effect would mix with the list of clauses. With this decision, `deprecated` is no longer a status value.
- **Keep only the half of R-003 that says "do not add".** Rejected: it repeats R-002 and P-001.

## Consequences

- The files of R-003 and P-003 are deleted.
- The step of the guide that cited R-003 now cites R-002 and P-001.
- Reference [9] shows that this repository no longer cites the result that only R-003 used.
- [3], the source of P-003, stays only as evidence for R-002.
